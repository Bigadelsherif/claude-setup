Homepage sections under the hero: a normal marketplace layout, only a bit creative, "dont overdo", "quality and luxury more than anything". The user rejected a gallery wall of framed pieces, a numbered catalogue page and arched niches on 1 November 2026 ("all is not good").

**Why:** the hero and header already carry the theatre; a shop page has to be easy to browse and feel expensive through restraint.

Superseded in part: on 7 Nov 2026 the client rejected the whole restrained redesign as not interesting enough. See [[client-rejected-redesign]].

**How to apply:** use familiar shop patterns (clean grids, tiles, rows of products) and earn luxury through photography, spacing, type and fine detail. Keep any creative touch small, one per section at most. Keep frames, walls, niches and heavy 3D out of these sections. This differs from the header and hero, where the user asked for depth and shadow.
