The owner wants the site's own drawings, lines and UI kept to grey, white and black (said 2026-10-18 about the About sketch's cream pencil lines: "i dont want this yellow").

**Why:** warm tints read as off-brand next to the glass look.

**How to apply:** use neutral greys (e.g. rgba(214,216,221,…)) for strokes and type in our own graphics. Real project screenshots keep their true colours (e.g. Lowkar's green) unless the owner says otherwise. See also [[owner-choices-over-audits]].
