**How to apply:** work section by section after the hero; don't touch Hero.tsx's scene. Concept for that art (2026-10-31, the user rejected scattering it in margins without an idea): THE ART IS THE SCENT. It only appears where scent does: it comes off a card, curls, drifts and fades (`puff`), the calculator's Plume grows with reach, each sleeve notch releases more, a faint Haze hangs in sections with no other art. Don't add decorative pieces without a reason from this idea.
"Don't be like a printer" (2026-10-31): never reuse a fixed shape. Every wisp is generated from random parameters and its curl is computed each frame; flowers are cut into petals that open and come apart; everything reacts to the pointer; each scent has its own style in SCENT_ART. Krea's role: the paint (watercolour washes that fill the live ribbons, repainted flowers in public/brand/scent, from scripts/brand/paint/slice_scent.py); its glossy pass looked like plastic, so use the hero's cockpit as reference and ask for flat gouache.
"I want a FLOWERY breeze" (2026-10-31): rooted plumes read as plants, and thin wind lines read as scratches (both rejected). What's wanted: masses of real painted blossoms and loose petals blowing across in waves, with depth. scentEngine.ts `Piece` (blossom/petal sprites from public/brand/scent, 25 Krea-painted, scripts/brand/paint/slice_scent.py) on one shared `wind`; `Breeze` sends waves through a space, scrolling pushes them and sets their direction; `puff` lifts them off a card; `GALE` + `fill` for the splash. Keep flowers away from key numbers (calculator mask).
The breeze's mix (2026-10-31, the user's request): about a third of the flowers are pale ghosts that dissolve on the way (GHOSTS share).

Grain belongs on the fresheners, never the backgrounds (2026-11-02, the user spotted it): the drive's shader grain and the site-wide film grain were removed; the shader's made a diagonal pattern on the night sky. The ask for samples is real paper feel (2026-11-01, tuned over several rounds): thick board (THICK 3.4), smooth matte surface with a fine even grain, pale cut edge, a gentle normal map, true colours (CardStage has no tone mapping). Too coarse reads as felt or sandpaper and turns whites grey; thresholded tooth reads as dirt flecks. Glossy artwork such as the Helix bottle needs its blacks lifted (MATTE in finish.py) and small type reset, or it looks fake.
