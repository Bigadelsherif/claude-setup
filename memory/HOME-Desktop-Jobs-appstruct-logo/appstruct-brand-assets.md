The AppStruct logo assets in `logo/` are pure monochrome: one filled path, `#000000` (white variants exist in `appstructlogovectors/svg/*-white.svg`). Mark = nested-chevron diamond lattice with a small "crowning" diamond above containing a plus sign. The shipped wordmark is spelled "APPSRTUCT" (R/T swapped) in every asset.

Brand colors are NOT in the logo files; they are defined in `../appstruct_website_system.md` ("editorial luxury, restrained metallic warmth"): bg `#0D0D0D`, surface `#171717`, bone `#F3EEE7`, muted text `#B5AEA4`, muted gold accent `#8F7A56`, optional deep olive `#4F5C55`. Rule: no bright gradients, accent used sparingly.

**Why:** The user asked for "brand colors" for the 3D sphere; the only authoritative source is that doc.
**How to apply:** Use the doc's palette for any AppStruct visual work; keep 80% neutral, gold as a single accent. See [[appstruct-3d-logo-lab]].
